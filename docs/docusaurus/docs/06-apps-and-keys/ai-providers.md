# AI providers and your own keys

By default, AI steps and agents use our AI providers and are paid for with credits. You can instead use **your own account** with a provider.

## Choosing a model

Every AI step and agent has a model picker. Models differ in quality, speed and cost, and some are only on higher plans. If a model is greyed out, check your plan, or whether it needs your own key.

## Use your own key

1. Open **Settings → AI providers**.
2. Choose a provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral or others.
3. Paste your API key, or sign in with the provider where that's offered.
4. Agent1o1 checks the key. Use **Check key now** later to recheck.

You can add several keys, set one as the default, replace or remove a key.

## Team keys and personal keys

- **Team keys** are used by everyone in the workspace.
- **Personal keys** are used only by the person who added them. Admins can turn personal keys on or off, and choose whether team keys also cover personal assistants.

When more than one is available, a personal key is used first, then the team key, then ours.

## Limit the models

Admins can choose which models the workspace is allowed to use.

## What it costs

With your own key, the provider bills you directly for the AI usage. Steps still use some Agent1o1 credits for running them.

## Keep keys safe

Keys are stored encrypted and are never shown again after you save them. Remove a key if it may have leaked.
