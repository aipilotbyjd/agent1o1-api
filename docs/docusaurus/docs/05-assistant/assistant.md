# Your personal assistant

The **Assistant** is your own AI, separate from the agents your team builds. It is private to you.

Open **Assistant** from the sidebar to chat with it.

## What it does

- Answers questions and does tasks using the apps you connect
- Sends you a **daily briefing** and prepares you for meetings
- Sorts your inbox and drafts replies (**Smart Inbox**)
- Reminds you about things you are waiting on
- Learns your preferences and writing style as you use it

## You stay in control

The assistant asks before it does anything that changes something, and you can set rules for each ability: allow, ask, or block. Thumbs up or down on an answer helps it improve.

## Daily briefing

Choose a time and what to include, such as your calendar, important mail and open follow-ups. Your briefing arrives at that time in your time zone. Turn it off any time.

## Meeting prep

Before a meeting, the assistant gathers who is attending, related emails and earlier notes, so you walk in prepared. Connect your calendar to use it.

## Smart Inbox

Connect Gmail or Outlook and turn on Smart Inbox (Pro plan).

1. New mail is sorted into labels like "needs reply", "FYI" and "newsletter".
2. For mail that needs a reply, the assistant writes a **draft in your own mailbox**, in your style.
3. **Nothing is sent for you.** You review and send.

Add, rename or remove labels to suit how you work.

## Talk to it anywhere

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| In the app | The Assistant page |
| Slack | Add the Slack app and message the assistant |
| Email | Send an email to the assistant's address |
| Text message | Verify your phone number (Pro plan) |

Everything you say in one place is remembered in the others.

## Recurring tasks

Ask the assistant to do something on a schedule or when something happens, for example "every Friday, summarise my week". These are your assistant's own triggers, separate from playbook triggers.
