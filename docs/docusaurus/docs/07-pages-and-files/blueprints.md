# Blueprints

**Blueprints** are ready-made playbooks, agents and pages. Copy one, connect your apps, and adjust it.

## Use a blueprint

1. Open **Blueprints** in the sidebar.
2. Switch between playbooks, agents, pages and collections.
3. Search, filter by **category**, and sort by **Popularity**, **Latest Added** or **Alphabetical**.
4. Open one to read what it does, then add it to your workspace.
5. Connect any apps it needs, check the settings, and run it.

Choose **Blank canvas** to start from nothing.

## Collections

Collections group blueprints for a goal, such as "Sales operations" or "Customer support".

## Make it yours

A blueprint is copied into your workspace, so you can change it without affecting anyone else. Rename it, change the instructions, add steps.
