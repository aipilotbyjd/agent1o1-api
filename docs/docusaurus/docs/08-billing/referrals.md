# Referrals

Open **Settings → Referrals** to find your referral link and code.

## How it works

1. Share your link or code.
2. Someone signs up through it. You'll see the visit, and then the sign-up, in your list.
3. When they reach the milestone (such as becoming a paying customer), you earn a reward.

The page shows your **link visits**, **sign-ups**, **credits earned**, **free days earned** and **rewards on the way**, plus the **next milestone** you're working towards.

## Choose where rewards go

Pick which workspace receives your rewards.

## Good to know

- Signing up with your own link or with a blocked email domain doesn't count.
- If a referred customer is refunded early, the reward is reversed.
- Reward amounts and rules can change. The Referrals page always shows the current ones.
