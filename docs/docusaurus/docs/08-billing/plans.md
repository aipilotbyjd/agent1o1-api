## Change plan

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Plans**.
2. Choose a plan and billing period.
3. Preview the cost, which includes the adjustment for the rest of the current period, then confirm and pay securely.

## Cancel

You can cancel any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. You can resume before then.

## Invoices and payment details

Open **Settings → Billing → Invoices** to see past and upcoming invoices. Use the billing portal to update your card or billing details.

## If a limit stops you

When you reach a limit, such as the number of playbooks, you'll see which one and what to do. Upgrade, or remove something you no longer need.

## If a payment fails

We try again and email you. If it can't be fixed within a short grace period, the workspace is paused until payment is resolved.

Only owners and billing managers can change plans.
