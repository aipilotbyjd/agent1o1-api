# Team members and roles

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role.
3. Send the invitation. They get an email with a link to join.

You can change a person's role or remove them at any time from the same page. Anyone can also leave a workspace they no longer need.

The number of members you can have depends on your plan. See [Plans](../08-billing/plans.md).

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace |
| **Admin** | Manage members, apps, settings and policies |
| **Editor** | Build, edit and publish playbooks, agents and pages |
| **Member** | Run playbooks and chat with agents |
| **Viewer** | Look, but not change |

Some actions need a specific permission, such as approving a request to publish, or managing billing. If a button is missing or greyed out, ask an admin to check your role.

## Several workspaces

Use the workspace picker to switch between workspaces. Each has its own playbooks, apps, members, credits and plan.

## Workspace settings

**Settings → Workspace** lets admins rename the workspace. **Settings → Tags** manages the tags you use to organise playbooks and agents.

## Activity log

Admins can review who did what in the workspace: sign-ins, changes to members, apps and settings.
