# Notifications

Agent1o1 can tell you when something needs your attention, for example when a run fails, an approval is waiting, or your credits are running low.

## Your preferences

Open **Settings → Notifications** and choose which events you want and how you receive them (in the app, by email, or in a channel).

## Channels

Admins can add shared destinations, such as a Slack channel or an email address, under **Settings → Notification channels**. Use **Test** to send a sample message and confirm it arrives.

## Announcements

News and maintenance notices from the Agent1o1 team appear in the app. You can dismiss them once read.

## Credit alerts

You can be warned as your credits run low. Set the thresholds under **Settings → Billing**. See [Credits](../08-billing/credits.md).
