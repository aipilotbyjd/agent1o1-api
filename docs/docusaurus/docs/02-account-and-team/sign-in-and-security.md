# Sign in and account security

## Ways to sign in

- **Email and password**. You must confirm your email the first time.
- **Google** or **GitHub**.
- **Magic link**: we email you a one-time sign-in link, so you don't need a password.

If you were invited to a workspace, open the invitation email and follow the link. Sign in or create an account, and you join automatically.

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password**. We email you a link to set a new one. For safety, resetting your password signs you out everywhere.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a code from an authenticator app each time you sign in.

**Turn it on**

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Start two-factor setup and scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
3. Enter the code to confirm.
4. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe. They are shown once.

**Lost your phone?** Use a recovery code to sign in, then regenerate a new set in **Settings → Security**. Regenerating makes your old codes stop working.

**Turn it off** in the same place. After that, sign-in only needs your password.

## Change your password or email

In **Settings → Security** you can change your password. You can also choose to **sign out of all other devices** at the same time.

To change your email, enter the new address in **Settings → Profile**. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and your old address stays active until you confirm.

## Locked account

Too many wrong passwords in a row temporarily locks your account. Wait a few minutes and try again, or use **Forgot password**.

## Your profile

In **Settings → Profile** you can change your name, photo, language and time zone. Your time zone is used for schedules and briefings.
