# Quickstart

You will start from a Blueprint, run it, and check the result in the Trail.

## 1. Sign up

1. Create an account with your email, Google or GitHub.
2. Confirm your email using the link we send you.
3. Answer the short welcome questions. We create your workspace for you.

## 2. Start from a Blueprint

1. Open **Blueprints** in the sidebar.
2. Browse or search, then choose one that is close to what you need. Pick **Blank canvas** if you want to start empty.
3. Add it to your workspace. It opens in the editor.

## 3. Connect your apps

Steps that talk to an app (Slack, Gmail, Google Sheets, and so on) need a connection. Open **Apps**, choose **Connect App**, and sign in to the app when asked. You can also connect from inside a step.

## 4. Run it

1. Open a step and check its settings. Fill in anything marked as required.
2. Choose **Run**. If the playbook asks for inputs, fill them in.
3. Watch each step finish. Select a step to see what it received and what it produced.

## 5. Publish

When the result looks right, choose **Publish**. Your playbook is now ready to start automatically (see [Starting playbooks automatically](../03-playbooks/triggers.md)).

## 6. Check the Trail

Open **Trail** to see every run: when it ran, whether it worked, how long it took and how many credits it used.
