# Organise your playbooks

## Find things

On the **Playbooks** page, search by title or description, sort, and switch between grid and list view. Filter by tag, or choose **All tags** to clear the filter.

## Folders

Create folders to group playbooks and agents, give them a colour, and drag items in. Rename a folder from its menu.

## Tags

Add tags to playbooks and agents, then filter by them. Admins manage the list of tags in **Settings → Tags**.

## Favourites

Star a playbook to keep it near the top.

## Duplicate

Use **Duplicate** to copy a playbook and its steps. This is the quickest way to try a change safely.

## Trash

Deleted playbooks and agents go to **Trash**, where you can search for them and **restore** them. Delete from the Trash to remove one permanently. Permanent deletion can't be undone.
