# Approvals

Some things should not happen without a person saying yes. The **Approvals** page lists requests that are waiting for you.

## What needs approval

- A playbook reached a **Human Approval** step.
- An agent wants to do something your [agent policy](../04-agents/autonomy.md) says needs permission, such as sending an email or deleting a record.
- A teammate asked to publish a playbook, if your workspace requires review before publishing.

## Decide

1. Open **Approvals**.
2. Filter by agent or status if the list is long.
3. Open a request to see what is being asked and why. For agent requests you can open the conversation.
4. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**.

If you have Slack connected for approvals, you can decide straight from the Slack message.

## Expiry

Requests that nobody answers expire after a time your admin sets. Expired requests are treated as rejected.

## Who can approve

Admins and the people your workspace allows. If you can't see a request, ask an admin.
